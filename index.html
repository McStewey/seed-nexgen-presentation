SEED Homes × NexGen Steel

Three compact homes adapted for fixed, site assembled steel framing. We propose purchasing engineered steel wall panels and roof trusses from NexGen. Our team will handle unloading, erection and the rest of the build on site.

Who does what

NexGen supplies

Price engineered cold-formed steel wall panels and roof trusses for each model, along with shop drawings, panel identification, packing, delivery and installation documentation. Identify included and separately priced connections, hardware and engineering.

Our team assembles

Coordinate the site and slab with the engineer, receive and stage the panels, then erect and brace the walls and trusses. Our team manages sheathing, enclosure, insulation, rough-ins, finishes and inspection coordination.

Pricing request for NexGen

  1. Provide a per-model price for panels, trusses and engineering, with a separate freight line. Show a single-home order and a combined three-home order.
  2. Identify crew and equipment requirements for unloading and erection, installation support, hardware, temporary bracing and the foundation connection details. Flag any specialty steel or work outside the supply scope.
  3. Price the simplest shallow roof that fits each concept. Show a simple 4:12 gable as an alternate where it changes truss cost. Our finish basis uses continuous LVP across compatible rooms.
  4. Confirm lead time, drawing inputs, quote validity and what you need from our local engineer before you can release the package.
